Company FAQ

What are your business hours?
We are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. We are closed on weekends and public holidays.

What is your refund policy?
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, as long as the product is unused. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we ship to over 30 countries. International orders typically take 7-14 business days to arrive, depending on the destination.

How do I contact support?
You can reach our support team by email at support@example.com or through the live chat on our website, available during business hours.

Do you offer student discounts?
Yes, students with a valid student ID get 15% off all purchases. Contact support with proof of enrollment to receive your discount code.
